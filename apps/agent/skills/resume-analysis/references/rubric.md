# Resume review rubric

## Evidence first

Score the document's communication, not the person's worth or hiring probability. Quote the actual section, item and field for a claimed defect. Separate a missing fact from a false fact. A short early-career resume can communicate well without business metrics. Each reviewer should explain the strongest reason for its score; the tool combines peer (30%), hiring manager (40%) and ATS/recruitment (30%) perspectives.

## Dimensions and anchors

Use 0–100 for the existing category keys. The anchors below are ranges, not precision claims. Choose within a range only when the evidence warrants it.

| Dimension                 | 0–29                                                     | 30–59                                                           | 60–79                                                                        | 80–100                                                                                                  |
| ------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| ImpactAndActionability    | Activities cannot be identified                          | Duties named, contribution unclear                              | Specific actions and deliverables in most relevant entries                   | Clear actions, personal scope and supported outcomes throughout                                         |
| QuantifiableAchievements  | Numbers conflict or are uninterpretable                  | Relevant scale or results missing context                       | Available metrics retain units and periods; nonnumeric outcomes are concrete | Relevant outcomes have traceable baselines, scope and periods, or equally specific deliverable evidence |
| ClarityAndReadability     | Structure or wording prevents understanding              | Repetition, unclear chronology or dense lists obstruct scanning | Consistent headings, dates and concise sentences with minor gaps             | Role, chronology and contributions are quickly understood; no material ambiguity                        |
| AiCollaboration           | An explicitly relevant AI claim contradicts the evidence | An explicit AI claim is vague about task or verification        | Where relevant, task, human contribution and checking are stated             | Where relevant, boundaries, verification and limitations are concrete                                   |
| CredibilityAndSpecificity | Material contradictions or unsupported claims            | Inflated language or ambiguous attribution dominates            | Claims are mostly specific with limited evidence gaps                        | Ownership, dates, qualifications and outcomes align with the supplied source                            |

Do not reward merely mentioning AI or punish a resume for omitting it. If AI work is irrelevant or absent, use a neutral AiCollaboration score of 50 and explain that it is not assessed. Metrics are not mandatory in every bullet: do not lower a score solely because a valid qualitative deliverable has no percentage. Never recommend making up data to reach a higher band.

## ATS readability checks

Inspect what is actually available. JSON or a text view cannot prove that a PDF's visual columns extract in order; mark that check unverified until an export is inspected.

- Standard, descriptive section labels; no essential content hidden only in icons or images.
- Name and contact text are real fields; email and URLs are complete and not broken by styling.
- Employer, role, school, degree and dates can be distinguished without visual position alone.
- Consistent date formats and reverse chronology; overlapping roles are clarified when necessary, never silently “fixed”.
- Bullets describe one contribution each; no keyword blocks, repeated synonyms or invisible text.
- Technical terms match demonstrated experience and use common spellings; abbreviations are expanded where ambiguity matters.
- Exported text, when available, has sensible reading order, readable characters, meaningful link text and complete headings.

ATS checks identify parsing risk, not a guarantee of passing any particular screening system.

## Common issues and practical fixes

| Issue                                                                   | Severity guidance                        | Fix                                                                                  |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Missing contact information, impossible dates, contradictory role facts | High if it blocks contact or credibility | Ask for the correct source fact; preserve uncertain dates pending confirmation       |
| Team result claimed as sole ownership                                   | High                                     | State the individual contribution and shared outcome separately                      |
| Duties without actions or deliverables                                  | Medium                                   | Ask what was implemented, changed, checked or delivered; rewrite only the known part |
| Metric lacks unit, baseline or period                                   | Medium; high if misleading               | Retain the number and request context; do not reverse-engineer missing data          |
| Long bullets repeat the same point                                      | Medium                                   | Keep the most relevant evidence and split distinct contributions                     |
| Unsupported superlatives or proficiency labels                          | Medium                                   | Replace with demonstrated scope or ask for supporting work                           |
| Inconsistent tense, punctuation or term spelling                        | Low unless meaning changes               | Normalize within the document language and preserve proper names                     |
| Role-specific relevance unclear                                         | Medium                                   | Request a JD for a fit review; do not invent requirements for a general assessment   |

Return a small, prioritized set of actionable issues. For each, state the observed problem, why it matters, a concrete edit or question, and any missing evidence. Do not duplicate the full report in chat and do not write the resume during an analysis.
